English:
We are excited to announce that Corpus Christi Parish is exploring the possibility of establishing a sister parish relationship with Ein Arik Parish in the West Bank, just north of Jerusalem. This initiative is being facilitated through the Order of the Holy Sepulchre, which has a mission to support Christians in the Holy Land. The Annunciation Church and school in Ein Arik are enthusiastic about this potential partnership, and we have been in discussions with Fr. Firas Abedrabbo, the parish priest, to explore various ways this relationship can mutually enrich our faith communities.

This relationship offers many opportunities for parish outreach, including youth and adult group interactions, pilgrimage destinations, and support for Christian schools in the Holy Land. The primary challenge facing Christians in the Holy Land is keeping schools operational. Schools are the heart of the Christian community, and for students, they represent hope for a better future. However, many families struggle financially and cannot afford tuition, leading to unpaid teachers and the potential closure of schools. Education is crucial for the future viability of these communities.

To address this, the Order of the Holy Sepulchre is connecting parish members with the Latin Patriarchate parish and school in Ein Arik, ensuring that any funding raised goes directly to the parish. We have several volunteer members from our parish who have experience with this model and are eager to lead this initiative. The core team leadership includes Matthew and Kristin Ward, Jennifer and Peter Schiller, and Jason and Donna Twitchell. We have also discussed the possibility of including Ein Arik in our upcoming pilgrimage, where we could spend a day together, celebrate Mass, and share a meal.

Fr. Firas Abedrabbo expressed his appreciation for our efforts: "Your desire to begin by supporting Christian education — especially through school scholarships — touches a very real need in our community. Many families here struggle financially, and helping children remain in a Christian educational environment is not only an academic support, but also a way of strengthening the living presence of the Church in the Holy Land. Beyond financial assistance, I deeply appreciate your vision of building a relationship rooted in friendship, solidarity, shared prayer, and mutual support. A true twinning is not only about resources, but about communion — walking together as members of the same Body of Christ.”
